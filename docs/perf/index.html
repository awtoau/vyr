The matrix

Rows are platform legs, columns are quality tiers, and each tile is one cell. render_only is the headline series — the total includes the benchmark's own FNV hash fold over every output byte, which is not rendering and was 36–55 % of a cheap frame before #44. Every tile carries a provenance chip saying how its render-only figure was obtained, because provenance being invisible is exactly how this project published a wrong number four times.

Why the ledger is a rebuild, not an append

Schema 3 (2026-07-24). Every M4 instruction figure here was produced by one instrument — scripts/perf-harness.py — replayed over history by scripts/perf-replay.py: old commits measured with today's tools, never with their own. The SYS_CLOCK-derived rows of the previous ledger were host wall time and are deleted rather than relabelled, and the benchmark's own hash fold is now a separate recorded field instead of being silently inside the headline. The four measurement errors that forced this are in perf-history.md.
